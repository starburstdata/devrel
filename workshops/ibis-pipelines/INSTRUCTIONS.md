# Workshop Instructions: Building Data Pipelines with Ibis and Starburst

This document contains the step-by-step instructions needed to complete the [Building Data Pipelines with Ibis and Starburst](./README.md) workshop.

## Setup

Follow the [Environment Setup](../ENV-SETUP.md) instructions prior to the next steps.

Then visit the [Jupyter notebook](./IbisWithStarburstGalaxy.ipynb) and click on the **Open in Colab** button at the top.


## Follow-along with presenter for hands-on activities

Presenter will land on slides titled "Hands-on" and/or "Demo" along the way and they will perform the activites along with you. You will primarily use the Jupyter notebook, but some activities will occur in the Starburst UI.

## Teardown

Be sure to follow the [teardown](../ENV-SETUP.md#teardown) instructions.
