# ai-ready-data-products

## Office Hours: Building AI-Ready Data Products

### [Watch On-demand](https://www.starburst.io/info/office-hours-building-ai-ready-data-products/)

### Description

This 30 minute Office Hours interactive & hands-on webinar explores how to promote curated datasets into AI-ready data products on Starburst. We enhance business-oriented metadata and then compare the quality of the AI agent's output before and after provide this additional context.
