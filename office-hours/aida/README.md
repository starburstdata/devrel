## Office Hours: Getting Hands-On with AIDA

### [Watch On-demand](https://www.starburst.io/info/office-hours-getting-hands-on-with-aida/)

### Description

This 30 minute Office Hours interactive & hands-on webinar walks through how AIDA enables natural language queries, assisted analysis, and AI-driven decision support directly on top of your data platform. We’ll cover how to get started, practical setup guidance, and how teams can safely operationalize AIDA for real-world use cases.
